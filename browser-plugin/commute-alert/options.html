Commute Alert

Know when to leave, or just keep an eye on traffic, using live Google Maps travel times.

How it works

When a check is due, Commute Alert opens your route on Google Maps in a minimized window for a few seconds, reads the travel time, and closes it. If Google asks you about cookies, open google.com/maps once and make your choice.

Notifications

Not seeing alerts? Send a test. If nothing appears, allow notifications for your browser in Windows Settings > System > Notifications, and turn off Do not disturb.
